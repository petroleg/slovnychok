Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

SlovnychOK does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. The app has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server of ours.

What stays on your device

  • Your cards, review history, notes, settings, imported texts and books are stored only on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Pronunciation recordings you make are kept on the device and are never uploaded.
  • The camera is used only for scanning English text with Live Text. Camera frames are processed on the device and are not saved.
  • Photos you choose to scan are opened through the system photo picker; the app sees only the photos you pick.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device.
  • Example sentences written with Apple Intelligence are generated on the device by Apple's on-device model.
  • The widgets and the Share extension read the same on-device data.

Backups

The app can save backup files of your data. Weekly backups are stored in the app's folder, which you can see in the Files app. Files you export or share go only where you send them. Your device's own iCloud or computer backup may include the app's data, under Apple's terms.

Links to other websites

Some dictionary links open other websites (for example Wiktionary or Cambridge Dictionary) in Safari. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

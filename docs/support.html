Support

Found a mistake in a translation, a bug, or have an idea? Open an issue at github.com/petroleg/slovnychok/issues. Please include the word or screen, your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.

Common questions

Do I need an internet connection? No. The dictionary, cards and exercises work offline.

How do I move my cards to a new phone? In Settings, make a backup and save it to Files or iCloud Drive, then restore it on the new device. A normal iCloud device backup also includes the app's data.

Why are some example sentences missing? Examples written with Apple Intelligence need a device that supports it, with iOS 26 or later.

Can I use the dictionary data? Yes, under CC BY-SA 4.0. Download it from the releases page.
